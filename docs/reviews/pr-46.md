Status: open.

Reviewed head: `862fd4c`.

Location: `WhatYouCarry.Tests/RepositoryShapeTests.cs:156-160`.

Trigger: Change the workflow comment from `08:07 UTC` to another UTC time while leaving `02:07 Central Standard Time` unchanged.

Expected: The regression test rejects that change because D-286 and D-285 require the cron to return to `08:07 UTC` after the schedule test.
