## Findings

### P2-1: A same-tick descent can build every enemy tree

Status: open.

Open at: `34c2ddf8c72702056b827dc552cd1ca9ebd262fa`.

File: `WhatYouCarry.Game/Render/EnemyNodes.cs:140-147, 181-185`.

Trigger: the worker completes its plan on a physics tick where the player descends. `Main` calls `Stage` before `loop.Step`, then `Rebuild` runs before any `DrawFrame` can call `BuildSome`.

Expected: the tree allocations stay out of the descent tick, as required by the F-193 optimization and the transition budget of D-635.

Actual: `TakeStaged` receives no trees, then `Grow` builds every enemy tree during `Rebuild` on the descent tick.

Consequence: a supported timing race can put the tree construction and its allocations back in the transition frame. The Deck runs cover a plan that arrived before the descent, but do not cover this race.

Correction: ensure a newly offered plan cannot make the descent build all its trees in the transition tick. Keep the descent behavior and tree visibility correct.

Regression check: add a test for a worker plan offered on the same physics tick as descent. Require the descent rebuild to build no enemy trees, and require all staged trees to match the new floor enemies.
