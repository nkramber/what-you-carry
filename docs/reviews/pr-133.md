## Findings

### P2-1: A nearby enemy can hold the clearer at the stairwell

Status: open.

Open at: `5cb1b0c89e552ebeb9e62e02a7907c9423af148c`.

File: `WhatYouCarry.Core/Bots/FullClearer.cs:172-181`.

Trigger: The clearer leaves while a living enemy's feet are within `Weapon.ReachCentimetres`, but the enemy's box stays outside the blade's vertical hit band.

Expected: The clearer must progress to the stairwell or drop a target that it cannot hit. D-439 gives the leave rule, and D-774 applies strikes during that walk. The roadmap review focus also requires a stall check for an enemy that the blade cannot hit.

Actual: `NearestLiving` selects by feet distance and weapon reach. `Strike` then repeats attack presses. The leave branch runs before `Stalled`, so it never drops an enemy that receives no damage.

Consequence: The enemy can keep the policy in place until the floor budget expires. This preserves the softlock class that PR-102 is intended to prevent.

Correction: Track progress while leaving and drop an enemy after the existing no-gain interval, or select only enemies the blade can hit.

Regression check: Add a deterministic test with an enemy inside the scalar reach but outside the blade's vertical band. The clearer must reach the stairwell without a softlock. Keep `FullClearerStrikesTheEnemyInReachWhileItLeaves` passing.
