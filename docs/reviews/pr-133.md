## Findings

### P2-1: A nearby enemy can hold the clearer at the stairwell

Status: fixed in `5e26c7f`.

Open at: `5cb1b0c89e552ebeb9e62e02a7907c9423af148c`.

File: `WhatYouCarry.Core/Bots/FullClearer.cs:172-181` at the opening review head.

Trigger: The clearer leaves while a living enemy's feet are within `Weapon.ReachCentimetres`, but the enemy's box stays outside the blade's vertical hit band.

Expected: The clearer must progress to the stairwell or drop a target that it cannot hit. D-439 gives the leave rule, and D-774 applies strikes during that walk.

Actual: At the opening review head, `NearestLiving` selected by feet distance and weapon reach. `Strike` repeated attack presses. The leave branch ran before `Stalled`, so it did not drop an enemy that received no damage.

Consequence: The enemy could keep the policy in place until the floor budget expired.

Correction: Track strike progress and drop an enemy after the no-hit interval. The author added this behavior in `5e26c7f`.

Regression check: `FullClearerDropsAnEnemyThatTheStrikesDoNotHurt` uses harmless weapons on seed 6. The current test reaches the bottom. The author also reports that this test softlocks on floor 1 at tick 10800 without the drop.

### P2-2: A hit on another target has no regression test

Status: open.

Open at: `86d9b9978ccf204b728f672447ae4153cbef8c32`.

File: `WhatYouCarry.Core/Bots/FullClearer.cs:412-427`; `WhatYouCarry.Tests/BotTests.cs:290-299`.

Trigger: Two enemies trade the nearest place from tick to tick. A strike hits one enemy while `NearestLiving` selects the other.

Expected: Any landed hit resets the strike stall count, including a hit on an enemy other than the one selected on that tick (D-776, T-3).

Actual: The health sum in `StrikeStalled` correctly detects a health loss on any enemy. The tests do not exercise this case. The harmless-weapon test cannot produce a hit, and the seed 9153 test does not assert a cross-target hit resets the count.

Consequence: The Gitar-reported defect can return without a test failure.

Correction: Add a deterministic regression test in which the nearest enemy changes and a hit on the other enemy resets the count. The test must fail at `5e26c7f` and pass at the reviewed head.

Regression check: Run the new focused test at `5e26c7f` and at the corrected head. It must fail on the former and pass on the latter. The current focused suite passes at both heads: `dotnet test WhatYouCarry.Tests/WhatYouCarry.Tests.csproj --filter 'FullyQualifiedName~FullClearer' -v minimal` passed 6 tests at each revision.
