## Findings

### P2-1: The bit-identity camera fold does not replay the record

Status: open.

File: `WhatYouCarry.Tools/BitIdentity/BitIdentitySweep.cs:208-235`.

Trigger: Run the `bit-identity` sweep. The record is replayed at lines 208-210, but `AddCamera` creates a new `SimulationLoop` at line 219 and steps the original intent list at lines 220-222. It then hashes that live loop's camera pose and aim ray.

Expected: The bit-identity job must replay a record with camera motion on all three platforms and assert one hash (Phase 1 PR-8 exit test 5, D-151, G-20).

Actual: The replay result contributes only its final simulation hash. The camera and aim values in the new fold come from a separate live run. The sweep can therefore pass while replay-specific camera inputs or replay traversal do not produce the same camera sequence.

Consequence: The required cross-platform replay proof does not cover the new camera and aim behavior. A later replay defect can pass this job while the PR-8 exit test still appears satisfied.

Correction: Fold the camera pose and aim ray from the loop that replays the recorded frames. Expose the smallest replay traversal needed to visit each replayed tick, or move the camera fold into that traversal. Keep the record and the camera fold on the same replay input.

Regression check: Update the bit-identity test or sweep assertion so it proves that the camera and aim values come from the replay traversal. Run `dotnet run --project WhatYouCarry.Tools/WhatYouCarry.Tools.csproj -- bit-identity` on Linux x64, Windows x64, and macOS arm64 and require one hash on all three platforms.
